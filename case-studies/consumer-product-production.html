CASE STUDY

Case Study: Scaling First Production Run for a Consumer Brand

A U.S.-based consumer product brand engaged a26 Products to manage its first overseas production run after proving demand through domestic prototypes and early traction.

Key Facts

Geography

Vietnam

Timeline

~4 months

Production Volume

8,000 units

Production Value

~$240,000

Overview

A U.S.-based consumer product brand approached a26 Products to manage its first overseas production run following successful domestic prototypes and early traction.

The client required factory identification, pricing negotiation, tooling coordination, quality control oversight, and shipping execution.

The Challenge

The brand faced several risks:

  • Limited overseas manufacturing experience
  • No established supplier relationships
  • High tooling cost exposure
  • MOQ pressure affecting cash flow
  • Uncertainty around landed cost and margin preservation

Without structured oversight, the risk of delays, quality issues, or margin compression was significant.

Our Approach

  1. Factory Identification & Vetting
    Shortlisted 6 manufacturers across Vietnam and China. Conducted capability screening and production capacity validation.
  2. Cost Structure Negotiation
    Broke down tooling, per-unit pricing, packaging, and freight. Negotiated phased production to reduce upfront cash burden.
  3. Sampling & Tooling Oversight
    Managed prototype revisions. Coordinated tooling corrections. Ensured spec alignment before mass production approval.
  4. Quality Control Protocol
    Established mid-production inspection. Conducted final AQL inspection prior to shipment.
  5. Freight & Delivery Execution
    Structured FOB terms. Coordinated ocean freight. Managed documentation and customs compliance.

Execution Details

  • Reduced quoted tooling cost by 18%
  • Negotiated MOQ reduction by 25% for first run
  • Identified packaging redesign that lowered freight cost per unit
  • Maintained timeline despite two supplier production adjustments

Production completed within 11 days of initial target delivery window.

Results

  • Preserved projected 62% gross margin
  • Avoided estimated $40K in tooling and pricing inefficiencies
  • Delivered 8,000 units with <1% defect rate
  • Positioned client for scalable repeat production

The client has since completed two additional production cycles under a similar framework.

Why It Worked

Manufacturing execution requires capital discipline, supplier accountability, and structured oversight.

a26 Products operates as a production partner—not a sourcing broker—ensuring alignment between factory output and brand financial objectives.
