Lead times explained: why schedules slip and how to plan better.

Lead time is not one number. It is a chain of dependent windows: supplier confirmation, material readiness, tooling, sampling, production, inspection, and shipping. If one link is optimistic, the whole date collapses.

When this matters

Use this framework before you announce launch dates to sales, investors, or retail partners.

Planning method

  1. Break the timeline into controllable phases.
  2. Add buffers for known uncertainty points.
  3. Identify the highest-risk dependency per phase.
  4. Track weekly with planned vs actual dates.

Common mistakes

  • Using best-case dates as commitments.
  • Treating shipping time as the only variable.
  • Not revising plan after sample revisions increase.

Questions for factories

  • What causes delays most often in this product type?
  • Which milestones are dependent on external suppliers?
  • How do you flag schedule risk before a miss occurs?
