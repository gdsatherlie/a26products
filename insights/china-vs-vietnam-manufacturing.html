China vs Vietnam Manufacturing

Choose region based on fit, not assumptions.

In this guide

  • What this means
  • Checklist/table
  • Common mistakes
  • How engagements run

What this means

Both China and Vietnam can be strong options depending on product type, supplier depth, and lead-time needs. The right choice depends on your requirements, not internet headlines.

Use a structured comparison model so teams evaluate both options on the same factors: process fit, capacity, communication, risk, and commercial terms.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing by anecdote only.
  • Ignoring supplier communication reliability.
  • Comparing quotes with different assumptions.
  • No transition plan from sourcing to execution.

How engagements run

Use a weekly update with milestones, risks, decisions needed, and next actions. Keep a decision log and escalate if timelines, tolerances, or quality thresholds are missed. This mirrors our homepage cadence structure.
