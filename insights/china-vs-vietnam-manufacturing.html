China vs Vietnam manufacturing: choose by fit, not by headlines.

Both markets can deliver strong outcomes when matched to the right product profile. The wrong way to decide is “country first.” The right way is “requirements first,” then evaluate which supply ecosystem best supports those requirements.

  • Decision matrix
  • Tradeoffs by scenario

  • Common mistakes

Country decision matrix

FactorChinaVietnam

Tradeoffs by scenario

If your product requires many specialized components and quick engineering loops, China may offer better ecosystem leverage. If your product is less component-dense and cost profile favors a specific labor structure, Vietnam may be strong. The point is not to pick a winner; it is to match supply structure to product reality.

Risk factors to evaluate

  • Component dependency concentration.
  • Communication quality and escalation response.
  • Capacity seasonality and holiday windows.
  • Tooling transfer and long-term flexibility.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing country before defining product constraints.
  • Using one quote as market truth.
  • Ignoring supplier-to-supplier dependency chains.

Questions to ask factories

  • Where are your critical upstream suppliers located?
  • What are your backup plans for material disruption?
  • How do you manage schedule risk when upstream delays occur?
