How to choose a factory

This guide is written for operators who need decisions they can execute, not just theory.

Where this fits in the a26 Manufacturing Accountability Model: Verify

Why this matters

Most manufacturing problems become expensive because ambiguity survives too long. This topic helps you reduce ambiguity early by defining criteria, owners, and decision checkpoints.

Use it as an operating control in weekly supplier conversations, not as a one-time read.

Toolbox: Factory vetting scorecard

FieldWhat to capture
ObjectiveWhat decision this tool supports
CriteriaMeasurable pass/fail expectations

Execution notes

  • Keep assumptions explicit and written.
  • Separate discussion from decision logs.
  • Escalate early when criteria are at risk.
  • Tie commercial terms to operational realities.
