How to choose a manufacturer without getting trapped by price-only decisions.

Strong factory selection starts with capability evidence, not quote spreadsheets. A low quote from a weak-fit supplier often creates more cost later through delays, rework, and defect fallout. This guide gives a practical vetting process you can run with your team.

When this matters

Use this before tooling deposits and before committing launch dates. Once you commit to a weak-fit factory, switching becomes expensive in both time and money.

Five-step vetting process

  1. Define critical-to-quality features and process requirements.
  2. Request evidence of similar product execution.
  3. Run communication and responsiveness tests over two weeks.
  4. Evaluate quote transparency and change-order terms.
  5. Run a paid sample with measurable criteria.

Factory Vetting Checklist

Common mistakes

  • Comparing quotes without comparing process capability.
  • Ignoring unclear assumptions on tooling and revisions.
  • Skipping reference checks and sample criteria.

Questions to ask a factory

  • What similar products have you run at comparable tolerance?
  • Which process steps are subcontracted?
  • How do you handle engineering changes after sample approval?
  • What is your escalation process when milestones slip?
