How to choose a factory

Good sourcing decisions come from evidence, not introductions. This guide gives a founder-safe validation process before deposits and tooling commitments.

Reading time: 16 min · Last updated: 2026-03-03 Verify

Who this is for

Teams comparing suppliers for a first run, or replacing a current supplier after quality/timeline instability.

Key takeaways

  • Price alone is a poor predictor of execution reliability.
  • Capability must be tested against your category, not generic claims.
  • Communication quality during quoting predicts production behavior.
  • Shortlist decisions should include explicit risk tradeoffs.

What to validate before shortlist

Category fit

Ask for recent, comparable production examples with similar tolerances, finishes, and packaging requirements.

Process control maturity

Request process checkpoints, in-line control methods, and who owns escalations when drift appears.

Commercial clarity

Require written assumptions for MOQ, scrap, tooling maintenance, and lead-time constraints.

Communication discipline

Track response quality: do they answer questions directly and document changes clearly?

Practical checklist (copy/paste)

[ ] Comparable category proof shared
[ ] Process checkpoints documented
[ ] Quote assumptions transparent
[ ] Tooling ownership terms written
[ ] QC scope and defect logic discussed
[ ] Escalation contacts confirmed
[ ] Top 3 risks logged for each candidate

Common mistakes

  • Choosing the fastest responder as “best operator.”
  • Ignoring packaging and logistics capability during supplier selection.
  • Assuming tooling ownership terms are standard.
  • Skipping a documented shortlist rationale.

FAQ

Is factory size the main quality signal?

No. Process fit and control discipline are more predictive than headline size.

How many quotes should I compare?

Enough to see patterns, usually 3–5 credible candidates.

Can I validate remotely?

Yes, but use structured evidence requests and document gaps you still need to close.

When should I remove a supplier from consideration?

When assumptions stay unclear, process questions are avoided, or revision ownership is vague.
