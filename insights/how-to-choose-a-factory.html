How to choose a factory without guessing.

Most teams pick suppliers too early. They compare quotes before they verify capability, process discipline, and communication reliability. This guide gives a practical vetting sequence and a checklist you can reuse across categories.

When this matters

Use this framework before tooling, before PO commitment, and before setting launch dates. A weak factory decision creates compounding risk that no late-stage project management can fully fix.

Vetting approach

  1. Define non-negotiables: process capability, tolerances, compliance needs.
  2. Ask for evidence: similar products, process maps, QC controls.
  3. Score communication quality: speed, clarity, and issue ownership.
  4. Run a paid sample with explicit criteria.
  5. Review quote transparency and change-order terms.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing by MOQ and price alone.
  • Skipping paid sample rounds.
  • Treating glossy presentations as capability proof.
  • Ignoring unclear change-order terms.

What to ask a factory

  • Which steps are outsourced and to whom?
  • What are your defect thresholds and rework procedures?
  • How do you handle engineering changes after sample approval?
  • What does a typical escalation path look like?
