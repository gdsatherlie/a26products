How to Choose a Factory

Factory selection should be evidence-based and documented.

In this guide

  • What this means
  • Checklist/table
  • Common mistakes
  • How engagements run

What this means

Choosing a factory is one of the highest-impact decisions in a manufacturing program. A structured checklist helps avoid costly rework and timeline drift.

The goal is not to find a perfect supplier; it is to pick the best-fit path with known tradeoffs and clear risk ownership.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing based on quote only.
  • Skipping communication-fit review.
  • Ignoring process maturity signals.
  • No documented rationale for selection decision.

How engagements run

Use a weekly update with milestones, risks, decisions needed, and next actions. Keep a decision log and escalate if timelines, tolerances, or quality thresholds are missed. This mirrors our homepage cadence structure.
