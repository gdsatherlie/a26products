About a26 Products

We are a manufacturing execution partner for teams building physical products. We are not a supplier directory, introductions-only broker, or lowest-quote trading desk.

Our role is to make critical manufacturing decisions explicit and testable before they become expensive in production.

How we work

Programs run through the same sequence: Define, Verify, Lock, Track, Release.

  • Define: clarify requirements, cost/timeline targets, and non-negotiables.
  • Verify: test supplier capability and operational fit.
  • Lock: control sample revisions and change approvals.
  • Track: run milestone visibility and owner-level decisions weekly.
  • Release: approve shipment only when criteria are met.

See implementation modules on Services and operating guides in the Insights hub.

Who we serve

  • First-time founders launching first production runs.
  • Small teams without in-house sourcing operations.
  • Operators inheriting unstable supplier workflows.
  • Brands moving from pilot runs to recurring reorders.

Where we operate

China, Vietnam, Taiwan, India, plus select U.S.-based support for coordination.

Recent programs: packaging, accessories, simple electronics enclosures, softgoods-adjacent components.
