ABOUT

We run execution work, not just advice.

A26 Products supports teams that need practical manufacturing execution. We focus on clear deliverables, clear ownership, and clear quality release decisions.

Mandate

Our mandate is to reduce avoidable production mistakes. We do that by organizing supplier validation, sampling, production tracking, and quality release into a simple operating flow. We make complex topics easier to understand for both expert and non-expert teams.

Operating principles

  • Use plain language and practical deliverables.
  • Record decisions so teams do not repeat debates.
  • Escalate early when key thresholds are missed.
  • Lock specs before production scaling.
  • Release shipments only with objective evidence.

What we are

  • An operating partner for cross-border manufacturing execution.
  • A team that builds and runs deliverable-based workflows.
  • A practical bridge between founders/operators and suppliers.

What we are not

  • Not a factory directory.
  • Not introductions-only sourcing support.
  • Not a legal or certification authority.

Who we serve

Emerging brands: teams running first real production programs.

Established operators: teams improving reliability, reducing drift, or adding sourcing options.

Why manufacturing often fails (plain English)

  • Factory chosen on low quote, not fit.
  • Sample rounds run without clear pass/fail criteria.
  • Spec changes happen without control.
  • Milestones are tracked but ownership is unclear.
  • QC criteria are defined too late.
  • Shipment documents are incomplete at release time.
