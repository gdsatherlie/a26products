ABOUT A26 PRODUCTS

Our mandate is simple: make manufacturing execution clear and accountable.

We help teams avoid preventable factory, sampling, and quality mistakes by running a documented process with defined owners and decision checkpoints.

HOW WE THINK

Five working principles

  • Plain English beats jargon: everyone should understand the plan.
  • Decisions must be documented: memory is not a system.
  • Problems are cheaper early: escalation should happen fast.
  • Samples are only useful with objective acceptance criteria.
  • Shipment release requires evidence, not optimism.

WHAT WE DO

Execution we own

  • Supplier validation and selection support.
  • Sampling and tooling oversight with clear checkpoints.
  • Production milestone tracking and risk management.
  • QC planning and shipment release governance.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

What to expect from us

  • We are not a directory that just sends intros.
  • We are not a factory and do not run manufacturing lines directly.
  • We are not legal or compliance counsel.
  • We are not a "set-and-forget" agency without reporting.

WHY MANUFACTURING GOES WRONG

Common failure patterns we see repeatedly

  • Factory chosen on price alone without capability verification.
  • Sample approvals done without written pass/fail criteria.
  • Specification changes made verbally and not tracked.
  • Tooling progress not monitored until deadlines are already missed.
  • Production delays accepted without root-cause accountability.
  • QC performed late or with unclear thresholds.
  • Final shipment released with unresolved quality issues.
