The a26 Manufacturing Accountability Model

A five-part model for reducing avoidable execution risk.

1) DefineSpec + targets
2) VerifyCapability + samples
3) LockSpec lock + change control
4) TrackMilestones + decisions
5) ReleaseQC criteria + shipment readiness

What goes wrong without structure

  • Resin or material swapped after sample approval.
  • Carton downgraded, transit damage rises.
  • Tolerances interpreted differently by shifts.
  • Packaging dimensions drift from approved dielines.
  • Tooling change orders stack up unexpectedly.
  • Lead-time slips hidden until late milestones.
  • QC criteria missing, release disputes follow.
  • Sub-supplier changes not disclosed.
  • BOM revisions live in messages, not records.
  • Freight readiness discovered too late.

Our philosophy: accountability beats optimism

Optimism helps teams start. Accountability helps teams finish. Manufacturing execution works when expectations, evidence, and decisions are explicit.

  • Clarity before commitment.
  • Verification before scale.
  • Change control before revisions.
  • Tracking before escalation.
  • Release criteria before shipment.
