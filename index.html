Category coverage + exclusions

Categories we support

  • Home goods and kitchen accessories
  • Personal care accessories and packaging assemblies
  • Softgoods and textile-based products
  • General consumer products requiring QC discipline

Categories we do not take / require review

  • Highly regulated medical and pharma programs (requires review)
  • Hazmat-heavy categories without compliance ownership
  • Programs needing legal certification services from execution partner

We are not a fit if…

  • You only want the lowest quote with no process checks.
  • You want introductions only and no ongoing oversight.
  • Specs are unstable and changes are unmanaged.
  • You need someone to decide what product to build from scratch.
  • You want to skip sampling/tooling discipline to save time.
  • Timelines are fixed but unrealistic and non-negotiable.
  • There is no internal decision owner available on your team.

FAQ

What is a manufacturing execution partner?

A partner that runs supplier validation, sampling, production milestones, and quality release with documented deliverables.

How do you validate overseas suppliers?

We use fit criteria, process checks, communication checks, and risk notes before supplier selection.

Do you manage sampling and tooling?

Yes. We manage sample rounds, acceptance criteria, and spec lock/change control before scale-up.

How do you prevent quality drift?

With QC planning, defect taxonomy, release thresholds, and ongoing milestone checkpoints.

Do you do QC inspections?

We manage the QC oversight framework and release criteria; inspection execution can be coordinated as part of the plan.

How do you handle change control?

Changes are logged with owner, reason, impact, and approval before they enter production baseline.

Can you work with our existing factory?

Yes. We can validate existing partners and build controls around current operations.

Which countries do you source from?

China, Vietnam, Taiwan, India, and select USA partners, depending on category and requirements.

Do you provide financing?

Working capital support may be available in select cases after scope and risk review; it is not guaranteed.
