ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLES (NOT CLIENT-SPECIFIC)

Sample approved, production drift appeared

Situation: Team approved samples, but early production units showed dimensional drift.

What we did: Added spec lock controls, clarified tolerance thresholds, and introduced first-article checks by shift.

Outcome: Production stabilized and quality decisions became less subjective.

Tooling quote surprises during revisions

Situation: Tooling quote lacked revision boundaries and change-order assumptions.

What we did: Reframed payment milestones, clarified included revision loops, and documented ownership/maintenance terms.

Outcome: Commercial ambiguity dropped and timeline conversations became more predictable.

QC criteria missing at shipment stage

Situation: Team reached ship window without agreed defect thresholds.

What we did: Built defect taxonomy, set release thresholds, and formalized hold/rework/release decision rules.

Outcome: Shipment decisions became evidence-based and escalation improved.
