MANUFACTURING EXECUTION PARTNER

We help you manufacture your product — end to end.

A26 Products is an execution partner for teams building physical products. We guide the path from idea to factory selection, samples, production, quality checks, and shipment release. You get a better supplier fit, fewer surprises, and a predictable operating path. We are built for first-time founders and operators who want clear decisions and clear accountability.

MANUFACTURING IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Six-step timeline

1

Define product

Set materials, dimensions, target cost, and must-have quality criteria.

2

Choose factory

Validate capability, communication, and realistic lead-time fit.

3

Run samples

Use clear pass/fail criteria and record all revision decisions.

4

Lock spec

Freeze approved details before production to prevent drift.

5

Manage production

Run weekly milestones, risk review, and escalation.

6

QC + release

Ship only when acceptance criteria and documents are complete.
