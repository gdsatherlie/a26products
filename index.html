Where first production runs fail

  • Samples approved, production drifts.
  • Tooling owned by the factory, not you.
  • Lead times slip without early warning.
  • QC happens after shipment, not before release.
  • Spec changes happen informally (no change control).

FAQ

Do you work with first-time founders?

Yes. We often support teams running their first production program and need practical guidance.

Are you a broker or a directory?

No. We are an execution partner with structured deliverables, not introductions-only.

What categories do you support?

Packaging, accessories, CPG components, softgoods-adjacent items, and simple electronics enclosures.

How do you validate suppliers?

We use documented capability checks, risk review, and fit scoring before shortlist recommendations.

How does QC and shipment release work?

Release decisions are based on pre-defined criteria, inspection outcomes, and documented disposition decisions.

What do you need from me to start?

Your current specs, target cost/timeline, quantity assumptions, and known risks.
