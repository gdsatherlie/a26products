FAQ: manufacturing execution and sourcing operations

What does a manufacturing execution partner do?

We manage supplier validation, sampling governance, production milestones, QC release criteria, and shipment readiness documentation as one operating system.

How is this different from a sourcing agent?

A sourcing agent often focuses on introductions and quote comparison. We deliver operating artifacts, cadence management, and decision accountability through production release.

Do you support factory sourcing in Asia only?

Primary coverage is China, Vietnam, Taiwan, and India, with select USA partners where category fit and risk profile require it.

What are supplier validation services in practice?

Supplier validation includes capability fit review, process reliability checks, commercial model evaluation, and documented shortlist recommendations.

What is included in production management services?

Milestone planning, weekly status reporting, decision log ownership, escalation protocol, and coordination around QC and shipment release gates.

How do you handle manufacturing quality control oversight?

We define QC plan, defect taxonomy, acceptance thresholds, and release criteria early, then tie shipment release to documented evidence.

Can you run only sampling and tooling management?

Yes. Engagement can start with sampling/tooling governance and expand to production oversight and reorder cadence support.

Do you provide guaranteed financing for production?

No. Working capital support may be reviewed in select cases after scope, commercial model, and risk feasibility assessment.
