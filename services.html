Services as execution modules

We scope work in modules so teams can start where risk is highest, then expand only when needed.

Modules

1) Discovery & Commercial Validation

When you need it: Early brief is unclear, targets conflict, or quote assumptions are unstable.

What we do:

  • Clarify spec intent and non-negotiables.
  • Pressure-test target cost, MOQ, and lead-time assumptions.
  • Map compliance realities and sourcing constraints.

What you receive: Requirements brief, risk memo, target bands.

How to manufacture a product · What is MOQ?

2) Supplier Validation & Shortlist

When you need it: You have names but no confidence in capability or fit.

What we do:

  • Run capability and process checks.
  • Score supplier fit against category needs.
  • Document top risks before commercial commitment.

What you receive: Validation scorecard, shortlist recommendation, risk notes.

How to choose a factory · Factory audit checklist

3) Sampling & Tooling Governance

When you need it: Samples are moving, but revision control and ownership boundaries are unclear.

What we do:

  • Set sample gates and acceptance logic.
  • Define tooling tryout and revision workflow.
  • Establish change-control expectations.

What you receive: Sample plan, revision log, tooling governance notes.

Sampling vs production · Tooling 101

4) Production Oversight

When you need it: Program is active and decisions are being made reactively.

What we do:

  • Maintain milestone map with owners.
  • Run weekly risk and blocker review.
  • Track change requests with explicit approvals.

What you receive: Weekly updates, decision log, escalation register.

Production milestones · Lead times explained

5) QC, AQL & Shipment Release

When you need it: Inspection is happening, but release criteria are unclear.

What we do:

  • Define pass/hold/rework thresholds.
  • Interpret inspection outcomes for disposition.
  • Document release decisions and sign-off logic.

What you receive: Release criteria sheet, disposition record, shipment gate checklist.

Quality control and AQL · Packaging failures

6) Reorder Support & Vendor Management Cadence (Optional)

When you need it: First run shipped and repeat cycles are becoming inconsistent.

What we do:

  • Set recurring operating cadence.
  • Review vendor performance trends.
  • Track recurring root causes and ownership.

What you receive: Reorder cadence worksheet, KPI review structure, issue recurrence log.

Reorder cadence · Change orders
