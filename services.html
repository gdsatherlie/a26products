SERVICES

Execution support for every stage from supplier choice to shipment release.

If manufacturing feels opaque, this page is your plain-English map. Each service explains what it is, what you receive, and which common risks it prevents.

SERVICE 1

Supplier validation & factory selection

What it is: a structured process to choose factories that can actually execute your product requirements.

What you get: factory shortlist memo, validation checklist, and decision recommendation.

Pitfalls prevented: choosing based only on quote, missing capability gaps, unclear ownership.

See related deliverables.

SERVICE 2

Sampling & tooling oversight

What it is: managing sample rounds and tooling progress with objective acceptance criteria.

What you get: sampling tracker, criteria matrix, and tooling milestone plan.

Pitfalls prevented: endless revisions, sample-to-production mismatch, hidden tooling surprises.

See where this fits in the process.

Related pages

  • Deep dive: sampling/tooling
  • Insight: sampling vs production
  • Insight: tooling basics

SERVICE 3

Production management

What it is: milestone planning, weekly reporting, and escalation management during production.

What you get: production timeline, weekly update pack, and decision log.

Pitfalls prevented: silent delays, unowned risks, and last-minute firefighting.

See operating cadence example.

Related pages

  • Deep dive: production management
  • Insight: lead times explained

SERVICE 4

Quality control & shipment release

What it is: defining QC thresholds and release requirements before goods leave the factory.

What you get: QC plan, inspection criteria, corrective action flow, release checklist.

Pitfalls prevented: subjective inspection calls, unresolved defects, shipping avoidable problems.

See common mistakes we prevent.

  • Deep dive: quality control oversight

SERVICE 5

Reorder cadence / operating system

After first production, we set a practical operating rhythm so future runs are faster and less reactive.

  • Recurring demand and capacity planning checkpoints
  • Update templates for milestones, decisions, and escalations
  • Continuous improvement loop for quality and lead-time performance
