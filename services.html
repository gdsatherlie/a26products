What is included / not included

Included

  • Structured deliverables and operating cadence.
  • Risk tracking and escalation logic.
  • Decision documentation and revision control.

Not included

  • Factory ownership or legal representation.
  • Guaranteed pricing/lead-time outcomes.
  • Unscoped ad hoc project management.

Client responsibilities

  • Provide target cost, quantity assumptions, and timeline constraints.
  • Respond to pending decisions in agreed windows.
  • Share current documentation (specs, samples, revisions, inspection records).
