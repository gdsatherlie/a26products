SERVICES

Execution support from first factory shortlist to final release decision.

Each module below explains what it is, what you receive, and which avoidable mistakes it prevents.

Supplier validation & factory selection

What it is: structured factory vetting before commitment.

What you get: shortlist memo, vetting checklist, and recommendation.

Pitfalls prevented: selecting factories that quote well but cannot execute.

Related insight: How to choose a factory

Sampling & tooling oversight

What it is: controlled sample rounds and tooling milestone management.

What you get: sample tracker, acceptance criteria matrix, and tooling risk log.

Pitfalls prevented: endless revisions, hidden tooling costs, unclear approvals.

Related insight: Sampling vs production

Production management

What it is: weekly milestone tracking with explicit owners and escalation.

What you get: timeline, weekly update pack, and decision log.

Pitfalls prevented: timeline drift, unresolved blockers, surprise delays.

Related insight: Lead times explained

Quality control & shipment release

What it is: pre-shipment quality framework and release gates.

What you get: QC plan, defect thresholds, release checklist.

Pitfalls prevented: subjective inspections and shipping unresolved defects.

Related insight: QC acceptance criteria

Reorder cadence and operating system

What it is: post-launch rhythm for repeat orders and continuous improvement.

What you get: reorder planning cadence and exception handling playbook.

Pitfalls prevented: reorders that restart from scratch every cycle.
