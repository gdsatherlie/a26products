SERVICES

Clear service modules, clear outputs.

Each service is designed to be easy to understand. We define what it is, who it is for, what you receive, and which common mistakes it prevents.

Supplier Validation Services

What it is: A process to pick the right factory for your product and constraints.

Who it’s for: Teams choosing new suppliers or reviewing existing supplier fit.

What you get:

  • Factory shortlist memo
  • Supplier validation checklist
  • Commercial model baseline

Common pitfalls prevented:

  • Choosing lowest quote without process fit
  • Missing capacity/communication risks

Sampling & Tooling Oversight

What it is: Structured sample rounds and tooling checkpoints.

Who it’s for: Teams preparing to lock specifications before production.

What you get:

  • Sampling tracker
  • Acceptance criteria matrix
  • Spec lock + change control log

Common pitfalls prevented:

  • Open-ended sample loops
  • Spec drift between sample and production

Production Milestone Management

What it is: Weekly operating cadence for active production runs.

Who it’s for: Teams needing better predictability and clearer ownership.

What you get:

  • Milestone plan + gate checklist
  • Weekly status report format
  • Decision log

Common pitfalls prevented:

  • Silent timeline slips
  • Unowned blockers

Manufacturing Quality Control Oversight

What it is: Quality framework for objective shipment release decisions.

Who it’s for: Teams that want fewer quality disputes and clearer release logic.

What you get:

  • QC plan
  • Defect taxonomy
  • Release thresholds

Common pitfalls prevented:

  • Last-minute quality arguments
  • Inconsistent release decisions

Shipment Release & Documentation

What it is: Final release checkpoint for quality and required documents.

Who it’s for: Teams approaching shipment windows.

What you get:

  • Shipment release checklist
  • Documentation packet readiness review

Common pitfalls prevented:

  • Incomplete release packs
  • Late handoff failures

Reorder Cadence / Operating System

What it is: Repeat-run structure to keep quality and decisions consistent over time.

Who it’s for: Teams moving from first run into recurring production.

What you get:

  • Reorder playbook
  • Revision and decision controls

Common pitfalls prevented:

  • Reorder drift
  • Losing process memory between cycles
