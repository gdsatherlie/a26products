SERVICES

Engagement Modules For Controlled Production Outcomes.

Our services are modular, but governance standards remain consistent across every engagement. Clients can begin at one module and expand as scope matures.

Module 1: Factory Validation & Commercial Modeling

Objective: establish a supplier decision baseline that is technically credible and commercially realistic.

Deliverables: requirement alignment notes, candidate factory assessment, landed-cost and MOQ assumptions, and an aligned shortlist memo for decision review.

What success looks like: you have a defensible supplier pathway with known constraints, visible risks, and realistic economic assumptions before commitment.

Module 2: Sampling & Tooling Governance

Objective: move from concept variability to controlled specification lock through structured sample cycles and tooling oversight.

Deliverables: sampling tracker, acceptance criteria by round, tooling readiness checkpoints, decision log, and finalized spec lock document.

What success looks like: production can begin with fewer interpretation gaps, reduced change-order risk, and a clear record of approved standards.

Module 3: Production Oversight & QC Release

Objective: maintain milestone control during active production and authorize shipment using predefined QC release criteria.

Deliverables: production milestone schedule, quality control plan, issue tracking and escalation log, and shipment release checklist.

What success looks like: timeline variance and quality surprises are reduced, and shipment authorization aligns with documented evidence.

Module 4: Reorder & Operational Cadence

Objective: convert first-run learning into repeatable cadence for stable reorders and improved planning confidence.

Deliverables: reorder governance map, recurring milestone calendar, change-control protocol, and cycle closeout summary.

What success looks like: each reorder cycle starts with clearer assumptions, faster alignment, and lower operational noise.

Detailed Deliverables

  • Factory shortlist memo
  • Commercial model sheet
  • Sampling tracker
  • Spec lock document
  • QC plan
  • Milestone schedule
  • Shipment release checklist

Operating Notes

Our work is designed for operators and investors who prioritize manufacturing risk mitigation and production governance over short-term appearance of progress. Engagement depth and sequence are tailored to product complexity, supplier maturity, and internal team structure. Working capital for inventory production may be available in select cases following scope and feasibility review.
